### Address: $D026AC

Stores the current RGB 565 foreground drawing color. See also [DrawRectBorder](../../../syscalls/all/DrawRectBorder), [DrawThickRectBorder](../../../syscalls/all/DrawThickRectBorder).

### Size: 2 bytes
